### EVID — evidence and ruling discipline
Hunt the claims a playtest record makes that nothing in the changeset can check: a verdict with no observation trace behind it, a trace whose reasoning does not actually reach the verdict cell it carries, a number quoted from a log, export or capture that was never preserved, a citation naming a preserved file but not the point inside it, a citation whose target is not in the same changeset as the record that cites it, an expected response that reads as if it were written from the observation rather than before the trial, a finding resolved inside the record instead of queued with what it blocks, and a ruling that is undated, attributed to a person rather than a role, or written somewhere the project's binding does not put rulings.
Do NOT report: the four structural properties `sitting-record-complete` owns — a missing or empty frontmatter key, an unresolving `sources` entry, an absent build-or-branch identifier, an empty verdict cell against a status that is not the unrun word — those are this pack's gate check, not a review finding; nor any judgment about whether what the sitting found is good news, which is the human's ruling and not a reviewer's.
